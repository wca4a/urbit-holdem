    How this works

    Open this page in another tab or on another device to add a player. The ship sends each browser only that player's two cards. Blinds are 5 and 10; everyone starts with 1,000.
    Before each hand the ship publishes a fingerprint of a secret it has already chosen. Your browser then adds a random number of its own. The deck is worked out from both by a public recipe. After the hand the ship reveals the secret and your browser redoes the shuffle to check it. The ship cannot pick or change the deck. It can still see it.
